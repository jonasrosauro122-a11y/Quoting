Training Guide

How trainees should use this carrier portal

1. Read every question carefully

Each field is written like a real carrier portal question. Do not skip fields just because they look small; quote accuracy depends on details.

2. Confirm risk facts before rating

Auto quotes require garaging, VIN, drivers, violations, prior insurance, and coverage selection. Home quotes require occupancy, roof, updates, claims, and hazards.

3. Watch underwriting flags

Referral or decline does not always mean the quote is wrong. It means the risk needs review, documentation, or different placement.

4. Save and print results

Save the quote for the trainer dashboard/history. Use Print / Save PDF to create a browser PDF copy for submission practice.
